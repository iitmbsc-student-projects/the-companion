Syllabus

The official course syllabus, transcribed from the IITM BS Data Science & Applications course booklet.

§ 1Course Description

This course introduces students to scikit-learn, a widely used Python machine learning library, providing hands-on problem solving experience with the methods and models covered in the theory course.

Lectures-Tutorials-Practicals-Credits: 4-0-0-4

§ 2Faculty

  • Prof. Ashish Tendulkar, Google Research (Visiting Asst. Professor, Department of Computer Science and Engineering, IIT Madras)

§ 3Week-by-week

Week 1: Pandas - dataframes, selection, manipulation; Statistics.

Week 2: Scikit-learn - data loading, preprocessing (missing data, scaling, outliers); Feature selection; PCA.

Week 3: Pipelines and transformers; Preprocessing (imputation, scaling); Feature selection and PCA; Class imbalance; Demonstrations.

Week 4: Regression and evaluation; Regularisation and tuning; Classification (NB, KNN, SVM, trees); Ensembles; Neural networks.

Week 5: Regression on California housing - linear, KNN, trees; Ensembles; MLP; Baselines and demonstrations.

Week 6: Classification - evaluation; Models (SGD, logistic, NB, KNN, SVM, trees); Ensembles; Applications (MNIST, abalone, iris).

Week 7: Evaluation metrics - confusion matrix, accuracy, precision, recall, F1; Multiclass; Large-scale ML.

Week 8: Support Vector Machines using scikit-learn

Week 9: Clustering (K-means, HAC); Neural networks (MLP); ML workflow; Recommender systems.

Week 10: Decision Trees, Ensemble Learning and Random Forests (Continued)

Week 11: Transfer learning (theory and practice); Neural networks recap.

Week 12: Unsupervised learning
