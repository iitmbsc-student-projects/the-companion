MS2001 · Diploma / Data Science · 4 credits

Syllabus

The official course syllabus, transcribed from the IITM BS Data Science & Applications course booklet.

§ 1Course Description

A significant share of data and problem statements in data science arises from the business domain. This course provides a foundational understanding of how businesses are structured and operate from a data perspective.

Lectures-Tutorials-Practicals-Credits: 4-0-0-4

§ 2Faculty

  • Prof. G Venkatesh, Director, School of Technology, Dhirubhai Ambani University
  • Dr. Milind Gandhe, Machine Intelligence and Robotics (MINRO) COE, IIIT Bangalore

§ 3Week-by-week

Week 1: Introduction to economics - economic flow, demand and supply; Spreadsheets; Spreadsheet functions; Economics applications using Excel.

Week 2: External data sources and survey methods; Exploring demographic data; Analysing demographic data.

Week 3: Market size estimation.

Week 4: Pivot tables; Data visualisation; Market share and aspirational data analysis.

Week 5: E-commerce - case study and data analysis; Key questions and data review; Pareto analysis (volume and revenue); Scatter plots; Trend analysis.

Week 6: Sales and trend analysis; Inventory and ledger metrics; VLOOKUP and pivot tables; Key learnings.

Week 7: Manufacturing case study - data (sales, production, inventory); Revenue and portfolio analysis; Regional planning.

Week 8: Production and inventory analytics - scheduling, scrap, OEE; Profitability and margins; Safety stock and reordering.

Week 9: HR and recruitment - sourcing, selection, ranking; Channel effectiveness; Diversity analysis; Onboarding.

Week 10: Fintech and payments; Credit case study; Nudge economics; Data and segment analysis; Behavioral insights.

Week 11: A/B testing; Credit risk analysis; Dataset exploration and review; Monthly trend analysis.

Week 12: Business operations and management; Spreadsheet-based data processing.
