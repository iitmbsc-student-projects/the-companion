§ 1Course Description

AI/ML systems are becoming increasingly pervasive in all facets of life. However, deploying large-scale AI without addressing ethical, legal, privacy, and societal concerns can lead to undesirable outcomes. This course introduces the fundamental principles of responsible AI deployment.
