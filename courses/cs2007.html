Syllabus

The official course syllabus, transcribed from the IITM BS Data Science & Applications course booklet.

§ 1Course Description

This course introduces the primary methods and models used in regression, classification, and clustering, and examines their properties to understand their suitability for different problem settings.

Lectures-Tutorials-Practicals-Credits: 4-0-0-4

§ 2Faculty

  • Prof. Arun Rajkumar, Wadhwani School of Data Science, IIT Madras

§ 3Week-by-week

Week 1: Machine Learning - learning paradigms; Representation learning; PCA.

Week 2: Unsupervised Learning - PCA limitations and complexity; Feature transformation; Kernel methods.

Week 3: Clustering - K-means algorithm; Convergence and cluster properties; Initialization methods (K-means++); Choosing number of clusters.

Week 4: Estimation - MLE, Bayesian; GMM and likelihood; Convexity and Jensen's inequality; EM algorithm.

Week 5: Supervised Learning - linear regression; Optimization (gradient descent); Kernel and probabilistic regression.

Week 6: Linear regression - evaluation, cross-validation; Bayesian modeling; Regularisation (Ridge, Lasso).

Week 7: Classification - K-nearest neighbours, decision trees; Generative vs discriminative models.

Week 8: Generative models - Naive Bayes (Gaussian, decision function); Limitations.

Week 9: Discriminative Models - perceptron (update rule and convergence); Sigmoid function; Logistic regression.

Week 10: Maximum margin methods - SVM, soft margin; Dual formulation; Constrained optimization.

Week 11: Soft-margin SVM - dual formulation and complementary slackness; Overfitting and underfitting; Ensemble methods - bagging and boosting.

Week 12: Loss functions - SVM, logistic, perceptron, boosting; Neural networks; Summary.
