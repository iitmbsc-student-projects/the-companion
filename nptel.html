NPTEL courses

The NPTEL courses the BS programme accepts for credit, from the Student Handbook.

Before you enrol

  • A 4, 8 or 12 week NPTEL course counts as 1, 2 or 3 credits.
  • Only the courses on these lists can be credited, and only the SWAYAM certification version of each.
  • Register for the NPTEL exam with your @ds.study.iitm.ac.in email.
  • A course can be credited once, and to one programme only.
  • The lists are revised from time to time, so check the handbook before you enrol.
