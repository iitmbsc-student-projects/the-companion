Updates

New resources and changes to the site, newest first.

25 November 2026

  • Site

    Planner by level, and a My Term dashboard

    The Planner now has a level dropdown (Foundation, Diploma, Degree, electives, PG) with your courses in two columns, and MA3015 Linear Models with Applications is in the electives. My Term is a dashboard: pick your courses from a searchable list, see the next exam first, and work out what you need in the end term.

  • Site

    Cleaner top bar, and Sign in on every page

    The top bar is a single row with Scam alert one click away and the Grade calculator under More. The Sign in button now appears on every page, so your progress and plans sync wherever you are.

  • Site

    Disclaimer on every page

    Every page now ends with a short note that The Companion is an unofficial student project, so always check the official handbook and grading document for the final word.

24 November 2026

  • Site

    Lectures for Compiler Design and Discrete Mathematics

    Compiler Design (39 lectures) and Discrete Mathematics (58 lectures, weeks 1 to 12) now list their lectures from the official IITM BS playlists.

  • Site

    MLOps lectures, week by week

    The 55 MLOps lectures are now grouped into weeks 1 to 12 and follow the order of the course videos.

  • Site

    Common problems on the project pages

    The MAD I, MAD II, ML Practice, Business Data Management and Deep Learning & GenAI project pages now have a Common problems section: viva slot booking and reattempts, eligibility, deadlines, similarity flags and report rejections, with the answers course staff gave on Discourse this term.

  • Site

    Scam alert: new providers and tricks

    Three providers added to the Scam alert list (SkillOrbit, Wayeva Innovations and Univox Academy), Corizo's entry updated, and a new section on tricks students reported this term: mails from student accounts, "mandatory" courses with payment links, and calls asking for your ID or password.

  • Site

    New NPTEL section

    A new NPTEL page lists the 289 NPTEL courses the BS programme accepts for credit, from the handbook's Tables 2 and 3, with search, a filter for courses open this term, and links to NPTEL and SWAYAM.

  • Site

    Degree electives grouped by area

    The BS electives on the Courses page are now split into Data Science, CS and Programming, Science and Engineering, Humanities and Management, and the mixed areas. Searching a group name, such as management, shows that group.
